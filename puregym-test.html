♠ OVERVIEW ♣ PROBLEM ♥ SOLUTION

Check in to PureGym in one tap.

A self-initiated redesign of the PureGym member app.

Role
Research, UX, UI
Timeline
December 2025 - January 2026
Tested with
7 gym members

Problem

Members used the app because they had to, not because they wanted to.

I asked members at the gym how they use the old app. Here is what they pointed at on the home screen.

01 · Gym search

“Search feels non-functional or misleading…”

02 · Account

“No clear login page… hard to change account”

03 · Home, Scan in, Find a PT

“Buttons are not visually obvious or discoverable… Buttons contain too much text”

04 · Scan in here

“QR code never shows up when you want to…”

05 · Everything under it

“I hate the hierarchy…”

“Efficient but very ugly”

*Real comments, quoted or paraphrased from member interviews.

Solution

Your gym, your code, on the first screen.

Scroll to walk through the check-in screen piece by piece.

01

Gym switcher

Save every gym you go to and swipe between them.

02

Just me / Guest pass

Side by side, not stacked. Testers picked this version.

03

The code is the home screen

Open the app and it’s already there.

04

Add to Wallet

Skip the app at the door entirely.
