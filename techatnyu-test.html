Overview

Two chapters: rebuilding the brand as Marketing Director, then still designing as President.

As President my role covers a lot more of everything, but I still try to stay hands-on with marketing. Together with our marketing directors Jaik and Jiyoon, we launched one of the busiest semesters Tech@NYU has ever seen, sometimes with up to 5 events a week. I designed every slide deck, and the Fall 2026 campaign posts and our Instagram were designed by Jaik, Jiyoon and me together. Back in 2025 I built the brand kit, the post layouts and the club website, which shipped and has since been replaced by a newer design.
